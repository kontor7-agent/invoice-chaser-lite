# Lite prompts: the first three chase steps

Copy a prompt into your AI chat assistant and replace every `[BRACKET]` with your facts.
Never paste source texts, NDA material or bank details. Check every date and amount before sending.

## P-01 Courtesy nudge (3-5 days before the due date)

```
Write a short, friendly courtesy email BEFORE an invoice falls due. Purpose: check that the invoice arrived, is approved and has everything their accounts team needs (PO number, vendor ID, correct billing entity), so nothing delays payment.

Facts:
- Contact: [CONTACT FIRST NAME] at [AGENCY]
- Invoice: [INVOICE NUMBER] for [JOB NAME / REFERENCE], [AMOUNT + CURRENCY]
- Invoice date: [INVOICE DATE]; due date: [DUE DATE] ([TERMS] days)

Ask exactly one question: is anything missing so it can be paid on the due date? Maximum 70 words.

Rules: use only the facts above. Do not invent dates, amounts, clauses or laws. No threats, no sarcasm. Plain words that a non-native reader understands. Write in [LANGUAGE: English]. Give me a subject line, then the email, signed [YOUR NAME].
```

## P-02 Friendly first reminder (1-7 days overdue)

```
Write a warm, brief first payment reminder. Assume it is an honest oversight. No blame, no apology from me.

Facts:
- Contact: [CONTACT FIRST NAME] at [AGENCY]
- Invoice [INVOICE NUMBER] for [JOB NAME / REFERENCE], [AMOUNT + CURRENCY]
- Was due on [DUE DATE]; now [DAYS OVERDUE] days overdue
- Invoice is attached again

Include the invoice details once. Ask them to confirm the payment date. Maximum 80 words.

Rules: use only the facts above. Do not invent dates, amounts, clauses or laws. No threats, no sarcasm. Plain words that a non-native reader understands. Write in [LANGUAGE: English]. Give me a subject line, then the email, signed [YOUR NAME].
```

## P-03 Second reminder, ask for a date (8-14 days overdue)

```
Write a second payment reminder. Calm, a little firmer than the first one. The goal is a concrete payment date.

Facts:
- Contact: [CONTACT FIRST NAME] at [AGENCY]
- Invoice [INVOICE NUMBER], [AMOUNT + CURRENCY], due [DUE DATE], now [DAYS OVERDUE] days overdue
- My first reminder went out on [DATE OF FIRST REMINDER]; their answer so far: [NO ANSWER / WHAT THEY SAID]

Ask them to send me the expected payment date by [REPLY-BY DATE]. Add one sentence: if there is a problem with the invoice, tell me what it is so I can fix it quickly. Maximum 100 words.

Rules: use only the facts above. Do not invent dates, amounts, clauses or laws. No threats, no sarcasm. Plain words that a non-native reader understands. Write in [LANGUAGE: English]. Give me a subject line, then the email, signed [YOUR NAME].
```
